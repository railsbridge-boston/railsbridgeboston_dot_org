RailsBridge Boston

Thank you

For 15 years, RailsBridge Boston introduced people of all backgrounds to the concepts, tools, and techniques of Ruby and Rails development.

Led by an all-volunteer team of seasoned and enthusiastic Ruby and Rails developers, our workshops helped make technology careers more accessible and welcoming.

We are eternally grateful to every donor, organizer, volunteer, mentor, student, and community partner who contributed to that mission.

The workshops are ending, but Boston’s Ruby community continues. Stay in touch by joining an upcoming Boston Ruby Group event, where it all began!
